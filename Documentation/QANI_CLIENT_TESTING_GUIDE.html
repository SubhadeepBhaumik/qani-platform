⚙️ Scoring & Qualification Results

What to Test

After screening, QANI scores candidates on 5 key areas. You can see scores and final decisions.

🎯 5-Area Qualification Scoring

Candidates are scored on:

  • Location Match - Does candidate location match job location?
  • Salary Alignment - Is candidate salary expectation within budget?
  • Qualifications - Does candidate have required skills/education?
  • Work Rights - Does candidate have legal work rights?
  • Experience - Does candidate have required years of experience?
Step 1: Open Completed Application

Go to Applications → Click an application with status "Completed" or "Progressed"

Step 2: View Scores Section

Scroll down to see the scoring breakdown

Step 3: Review Individual Scores

Each area shows: Raw Score, Weighted Score, and Status (Pass/Fail/Review)

Step 4: Check Final Decision

See the overall decision: PROGRESS, REVIEW, or REJECT

Scoring Logic: Each area is weighted based on importance. The system automatically calculates an overall score and recommends action (Progress/Review/Reject).
✅ Expected Result: You should see scores for each qualification area and an overall recommendation
